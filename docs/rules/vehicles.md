# Vehicles, stickers and parking (Step 25)

- Change vehicles only through `VehiclesService`, stickers through `StickerActionsService` (reads in `StickersService`), slots through `ParkingService` (`src/vehicles`); module `vehicles`, area-scoped through the vehicle's unit (a slot through its own location). Each service writes its own audit rows in the same transaction.
- A vehicle belongs to a unit and to a person who owns, rents or lives in it now, or to an organisation that owns or rents it (migration `34_vehicle_organisations`: `vehicles.person_id` or `organisation_id`, exactly one, `vehicles_holder_check`). `checkHolder` (409 `vehicle_person_not_at_unit`) runs again when the unit or holder changes and when a sticker is issued, renewed or replaced. `GET /vehicles/unit-people` lists who may be picked, people and organisations. Certificates still take people only (their own `unitPeople`).
- Registration numbers match on `reg_key` (`regNoKey` in shared: English digits, no spaces or dashes, capitals), unique per society among vehicles on file. Vehicles are never deleted: removing one (`vehicles.delete`, with a reason) revokes its sticker, frees its slot and frees its number.
- Stickers: one active per vehicle (partial unique index). Expired is derived (`stickerStateOf`), never stored. Replacing marks the old one `replaced` and gives a new number to the same last day; renewing gives a new number to the end of the next sticker year (`renewalValidUntil`). Bulk renew skips each sticker that fails with the code its single renewal would give.
- Sticker numbers come from `NumberingService` kind `sticker` with the prefix from the setting; the prefix is never in code.
- The setting `vehicle_stickers` (`stickerSettingsOf`: prefix, `calendar | fiscal` validity, fee or null) is read with `vehicles.view` and changed with `settings.edit` (`/vehicles/settings`). Free and to 31 December by default. With a fee, issuing, renewing and replacing bill the unit through `InvoicesService.issueUnitBillIn` in the same transaction, and need `billing.create` there (403 `sticker_fee_needs_billing`).
- `GET /vehicles/lookup?plate=` gives a guard only the number, kind, unit and sticker, never people. It is not gate management: nothing is logged. The web has no lookup screen yet (Anis, 2026-10-13).
- Parking slot codes are unique per place among slots in use; a slot is held by a unit or by one vehicle (never both), and a vehicle holds at most one slot.
- Web: `/dashboard/vehicles` (tabs Vehicles, Stickers, Parking; `src/lib/api/vehicles.ts`, `components/vehicles/`). Stickers are issued, renewed, replaced and revoked on the vehicle's page; the Stickers tab renews the ticked ones together, prints those in force on A4 (`/dashboard/vehicles/stickers/print?ids=`), exports CSV and holds the settings card. `VehiclesPanel` sits on unit, person and organisation pages.
